
# Nova Retail Markets – Sales & Profit Analysis

A Business Intelligence project analyzing **Nova Retail Markets** sales performance (2023–2026) to uncover insights into revenue,
profitability, discounts, product performance, store performance, and sales trends.

## Key Insights

- **239.4M SAR** Total Revenue
- **79.4M SAR** Total Profit
- **33%** Overall Profit Margin
- **1,995 SAR** Average Order Value (AOV)
- **89.9%** Completed Order Rate

### Business Findings

- **Sports & Outdoors** generated the highest profit (~12M SAR).
- **Beauty & Personal Care** and **Books & Stationery** achieved the highest profit margin (**34.7%**).
- **Toys & Games** recorded the lowest profit margin (**31.1%**).
- **Khobar Branch** was the top-performing store in both revenue and profit.
- **Riyadh Airport Branch** recorded the lowest revenue and profit among all branches.
- Higher discounts **did not increase sales volume** and reduced overall profitability.
- Sales peaked in **July 2025 (11M SAR)**, while **January 2023 (145K SAR)** was the lowest-performing month.
- Sales consistently performed better during the **July–December** period.

## Tools & Technologies

- **PostgreSQL** – Data extraction & analysis
- **Power BI** – Interactive dashboard development
- **DAX** – KPIs and calculated measures
- **PowerPoint** – Business presentation & storytelling

## Dashboard Highlights

- Revenue, Profit & Profit Margin KPIs
- Average Order Value (AOV)
- Order Completion Rate
- Product & Category Performance
- Branch Performance
- Discount Impact Analysis
- Monthly Sales Trends

## Business Recommendations

- Increase inventory for high-performing categories.
- Apply targeted discounts instead of broad promotions.
- Use the Khobar Branch as a performance benchmark.
- Prepare inventory and staffing ahead of the July–December peak season.
- Continuously monitor monthly sales trends to optimize business decisions.
